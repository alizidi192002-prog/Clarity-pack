How to Make a Budget for 2027: A 6-Step Plan for the Whole Year

Updated 2026-09-30 · 7 min read

A yearly budget isn't twelve copies of the same month. Rent stays the same, but insurance renewals, birthdays, holidays and car repairs land in specific months. Plan those now and 2027 has far fewer surprises.

1. Start from what you really spent in 2026

Look at the last three months of bank statements and group spending into categories: housing, food, transport, bills, fun, and so on. Real numbers beat guesses. Most people find one or two categories much higher than they thought.

2. Set your monthly income baseline

Use your take-home pay after tax. If your income changes month to month, use your lowest normal month as the baseline and treat anything above it as a bonus you assign to goals.

3. Choose two or three goals for the year

Keep it short and specific: "save $3,000 emergency fund", "pay off the $2,400 credit card", "put $150 a month into a holiday fund". Divide each goal by 12 and put it in the budget as a fixed line, like a bill.

4. Map the irregular expenses month by month

This is the step most budgets skip. Go through the calendar and write down what's coming:

MonthIrregular expenseAmount
JanuaryAnnual subscription renewals$120
MarchCar insurance$540
JuneSummer trip$900
SeptemberBack to school$300
DecemberHolidays & gifts$600
Total$2,460

$2,460 ÷ 12 = $205 a month. Set that aside every month in a separate "sinking fund", and those months stop being a shock.

5. Build each month: budget vs actual

For every category, write what you plan to spend, then fill in what you actually spent as the month goes. The difference tells you where to adjust next month. A simple rule: if a category is over for two months in a row, the budget was unrealistic, so raise it and cut somewhere else.

6. Review once a month (15 minutes)

At the end of each month, check three numbers: total spent vs planned, how much you saved, and progress on each goal. Your yearly savings rate (saved ÷ income) is the single best number to watch over 2027.

Tip: start in December. Setting up the categories and irregular expenses before January means you begin the year already on plan.
